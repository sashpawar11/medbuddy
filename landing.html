Built for the real mess of family healthcare.

A discharge summary on your laptop. A lab report in Downloads. A prescription photo sent in a family chat. MedBuddy brings the fragments together without turning your health history into someone else’s dataset.

01

One vault, separate profiles

Keep records for yourself, children, partners, and aging parents organized under strictly separated family profiles.

02

Search the scans, not just filenames

Offline OCR extracts text from PDFs and common image formats, making old scans and phone photos easier to find.

03

Answers tied to their sources

Ask questions inside one profile and trace answers back to cited document pages instead of trusting an ungrounded summary.

04

See change across years

Bring test results, appointments, and medication updates into a chronological timeline that makes long-term patterns easier to review.

05

Prepare for the next appointment

Generate a structured overview with extracted metrics, plain-language context, and useful questions to raise with a clinician.

06

Revisit without paying twice

Generated analyses are cached locally, so previously created overviews open instantly without reprocessing the same files.

Privacy you can see

Local first isn’t a setting. It’s the default boundary.

Your documents, extracted text, profiles, and local database stay on your machine. Use Ollama or LM Studio for offline analysis, or explicitly choose a bring-your-own-key cloud provider when you want one.

There is no telemetry or remote logging. Optional Google Drive backup is encrypted and opt-in.
